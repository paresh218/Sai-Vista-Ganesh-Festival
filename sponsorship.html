Sponsor Sai Vista Marathon 2026

Connect with our neighbourhood through a shared morning of movement.

Sai Vista Cultural Committee welcomes sponsorship enquiries for the 3K and 5K Sai Vista Marathon on Sunday, 29 November 2026. The event starts and finishes at Sai Vista Society in Rahatani, PCMC, Pune, with a route along Aundh–Ravet BRTS Road.

Discuss sponsorship · 8149525915

The community and the event

Sai Vista Society comprises 305+ flats. The marathon has a combined limit of 200 participant registrations across both distances. This is event capacity, not a claim that all places are filled or a guarantee of spectator attendance, enquiries or sales.

The run brings neighbours, families, friends and volunteers together. It offers a relevant setting for businesses connected with fitness, nutrition, healthy food, hydration, sportswear, footwear, gyms and wellness. Other businesses can discuss a suitable contribution with the committee.

Ways to support the marathon

  • Financial sponsorship towards agreed event needs.
  • Suitable packaged snacks, refreshments or hydration products.
  • Fitness accessories, participant-kit items or relevant vouchers.
  • A combination of financial and product support.

Before confirming a contribution, we will discuss quantities, suitability, delivery dates, handling requirements and how items will be distributed. Please contact the committee before sending products or making a payment.

Brand recognition and participation

Depending on the agreed contribution, available space and production deadlines, the committee can discuss website recognition, selected event-material branding, announcements, participant-kit materials, or an appropriate display and sampling arrangement. T-shirt and banner placements must be confirmed before production; they are not guaranteed by making an enquiry.

A dedicated offer code or QR link may help a sponsor assess interest where agreed. Private resident information and participant contact lists are not provided as a sponsorship benefit. Paid placements will be identified as sponsored, and recognition does not imply a committee endorsement of a product.
