About Sai Vista Cultural Committee

Community celebrations at Sai Vista Society, Rahatani, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune.

Sai Vista Cultural Committee brings neighbours, families and friends together through community events and cultural celebrations. Our residential society has 305+ flats. This website shares committee event information, schedules, registration details and festival memories, alongside protected services for verified residents.

This is the committee’s community website. It is not a property-sales or real-estate booking website.

Community events and festival memories

The Sai Vista Marathon 2026 takes place on Sunday, 29 November, with 3 km and 5 km distances along Aundh–Ravet BRTS Road. Participants report at 5:45 AM IST at Sai Vista Multipurpose Court. All ages are welcome; children aged 12 and under must be accompanied by a parent throughout the run, and participants under 18 need guardian consent.

Our community portal also includes a public glimpse of Navratri 2026 and Ganesh Festival 2026 memories and programme information. Past-event information is kept as an archive, so check dates before treating a schedule or registration notice as current.

Public information and resident access

You can read this page, our marathon event guide and sponsorship information without signing in. The main community portal asks visitors to sign in. Google sign-in is available, with email-code verification as an alternative for other email addresses.

Only verified residents can access community accounts, member payment records, private photo albums and restricted downloads. A flat number alone does not establish residency. The committee checks the signed-in email against private resident records, and unmatched requests need committee review. Existing saved access is reused where valid; public search pages do not disclose the resident directory or individual registration details.

Read our privacy and advertising notice for information about verification, activity records, external services and your choices.

Supporting the community run

Businesses can discuss financial sponsorship, suitable products, refreshments or participant-kit contributions for the marathon. Recognition and event arrangements depend on what is agreed with the committee and available production time. See our sponsorship opportunities in PCMC for details.

Contact and corrections

For marathon and sponsorship questions, contact Paresh on 8149525915 or saivistaculturalcommittee@gmail.com. For other event co-ordinators, visit the committee contact section in the community portal.

For a correction, privacy request or question about your submission, contact the committee directly. Never publish payment screenshots, resident records or private contact lists in public comments.
