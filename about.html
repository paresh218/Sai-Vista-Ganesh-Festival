About Sai Vista Cultural Committee

Sai Vista Cultural Committee brings neighbours, families and guests together through community events and cultural celebrations.

What you can explore

Our website shares upcoming events, festival schedules, public video glimpses and memories from Ganesh Festival and Navratri. The 2026 Sai Vista Marathon takes place on 29 November, with 3 km and 5 km distances for participants aged 12 and above.

See marathon details and registration, watch the public Navratri glimpse, or explore the Ganesh Festival archive.

Guests and residents

Guests can browse public content without entering a flat number. Community accounts, resident payment records, photo albums and full festival video downloads are hidden in guest mode. Resident access requires a registered flat and email, a private committee reference check and an email verification code. Verified access is remembered on the same browser for 90 days.

Contact the committee

For event questions, corrections, privacy requests or business features, use our committee contact section. For advertising enquiries, contact Priyank on WhatsApp. WhatsApp is an external service.
