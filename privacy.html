Sai Vista Cultural Committee

Privacy and advertising

Updated 1 November 2026. This website is maintained for Sai Vista Cultural Committee community activities.

Guest browsing

You can view public events and memories without providing a flat number. Hosting and third-party services may process technical request data. Fonts, public media, event services and other connected features can contact external providers when used.

Resident visits

Resident access requires your registered flat and email, an OTP sent to that email, and a match against the committee’s private resident reference. Verified access is remembered on this browser for 90 days and checked with the server on return visits. Partial or family names may require committee review. When connected, the visit service records the server-verified email, resident name, flat, sections viewed, selected actions, date/time, site release and browser/device details for 90 days. The existing private email list also stores first resident verification and last resident activity timestamps. Historic flat-only records remain unverified. IP collection depends on the configured connection; the resident entry notice states whether it applies. A flat number alone does not grant resident access.

Private photos and video

Private media access uses your name, email, flat, verification code and consent. The gallery records access attempts, photo/video requests, view mode, download requests, time and browser information for 90 days. It does not collect IP addresses or hardware identifiers. Verification may be remembered for 90 days. Optional local photo copies may remain for up to seven days; your browser may remove them sooner. Sign out or use “Change flat / forget this device” on shared devices.

Event entries and community submissions

Event forms collect the information shown on the form for committee administration. Marathon entries include contact details, emergency details, optional medical information and payment evidence. Provide only what is requested and keep unrelated financial details out of screenshots. Contact the committee about access, correction, retention or deletion of your submission. Approved comments and community submissions may appear publicly; do not submit private information.

Advertising

Google advertising is currently disabled. If enabled following approval, Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage and identifiers to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where permitted and consented, personalise advertising based on visits to this site and other sites. See Google’s advertising data policy and My Ad Center. Required consent controls must be available before ads load. Declining advertising must not prevent public browsing. Paid community placements must be labelled as sponsored.

Your choices and contact

You can clear browser data, sign out of private media or forget your resident flat. External links such as WhatsApp and Google have their own policies. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the committee. The committee must review this notice when services or data practices change.
