Visarjan programme with evening DJ as noted in the latest festival schedule.

📌 Important Notes
  1. 14 Sep: Miravnuk is planned between 2–6 PM with music. Residents should plan work arrangements accordingly.
  2. 25 Sep: Visarjan will include DJ in the evening.
  3. Talent Show: Open for children of all ages; singing, dancing, drama, etc. are included. Sessions are across 19, 21 Sep.
  4. Performance day allotment will be done purely by draw; no changes will be allowed after allotment.
  5. Entries are limited, so residents are requested to register early wherever registration is applicable.
  6. Fancy Dress is a separate event on 22 Sep.
  7. 23 Sep currently has no activity planned; the schedule may be updated.
  8. Any schedule concerns should be raised with the committee by Thursday so they can be reviewed based on resident impact.
  9. Volunteers are welcome to contribute, manage or help plan events and activities.
